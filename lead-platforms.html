Category 3 • Staffing marketing / CRM systems

Skip All Four For Lead-Gen

These sell candidate attraction and client management — none provides venue contact data. Your bottleneck is finding venue decision-makers, which none of them solve.

Bullhorn — Starter $99/user/mo, Core $165/user/mo

ATS + recruitment CRM. The sales CRM is Pro-only (quote-only). Fits alone on Starter, but it's an ATS to manage leads you already have — only worth it later as the operating system.

Haley Marketing — Quote only

Staffing websites, SEO/PPC, career portal, marketing automation. Lead-gen is inbound — candidate applications + client enquiries, not venue clients. Only hard number found: Starter Site valued at $7,495. Monthly rate not verified.

Staffing Future — Quote only

SEO career sites / job boards synced to Bullhorn + 20 ATSs; “Future Loop” reactivates your own database. FAQ states pricing depends on site, team size, and features — “you'll get a clear quote on your call.” Project + hosting model, not a $200/mo tool.

Sense — From $500/mo

Candidate engagement / talent CRM: SMS/WhatsApp journeys, AI chatbot + Voice AI screening, database reactivation. Candidate-side only, no venue prospecting. Published starting prices (G2 / sensehq.com): Messaging $500/mo, Scheduling $500/mo, Platform $2,000/mo, AI Recruiter $3,000/mo, Talent Acquisition $7,000/mo. No free plan/trial. Cheapest module is 2.5× budget.

If a CRM is needed later, Bullhorn Starter ($99, 1 user) or a free HubSpot CRM are the only sub-$200 options — that's an ops decision, not lead-gen.

  • Lusha credits conflict: 5 credits/phone + 40 free/mo (most sources) vs. 10 credits/phone + 70 free/mo (one 2026 source). Re-check lusha.com/pricing.

  • Haley & Staffing Future: no monthly rate published anywhere found.
  • Seamless / ZoomInfo / Cognism prices are third-party reported, vary 20–40% between sources — order-of-magnitude only.
  • Google Places licence: whether building an outreach list breaches Google Maps Platform terms was flagged in secondary sources; the terms themselves were not read in full. Read them before bulk-building a list.
  • Small-venue match rates are unknown: no source confirms coverage for small independent Florida bars/clubs in Apollo/Lusha/UpLead. The free-tier test against ~50 known venues is the only way to know.
  • Still unspecified: how many venues/month you want to contact, and whether “verified” means SMTP-verified, human-confirmed, or website-published — that decides which tier is enough.

How To Run The Free Test (Before Any Spend)

  • 1. Build the venue list: pull Winter Haven / Orlando / Tampa bars & clubs from Google Places free tier; cross-check each against Florida DBPR — keep only active licences (4COP for full-liquor).
  • 2. Enrich ~50 venues: run the same 50 through Apollo Free (75 credits), Hunter Free (50), and Snov Free (50). Compare who finds an owner/GM email.
  • 3. Verify before any send: Hunter Verifier (0.5 credit) on every address. Count bounces/unknowns per tool — that's your real match + accuracy rate.
  • 4. Decide on evidence: if Apollo's match rate holds, Apollo Basic $49 is the first paid dollar. If you need volume of generic venue inboxes instead, Scrap.io Basic $39 is first. Don't buy both until the test says so.

Note: Steps 1–4 are the research report's recommended sequence, restated — no platform account was created during research.

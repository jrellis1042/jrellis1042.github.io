Good Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How Does Nocturnal Work For Venues?

Register your venue, post the shifts you need covered, review verified talent from the Rotation, then interview and hire direct. You stay in charge of who works your room.

How Do I Post A Shift?

Register through the venue form, then tell us the roles, the nights and the headcount. One post can cover the whole weekend — no reposting the same shift five times.

Is Registration Free?

Yes. Registration is free forever, for venues and for talent. There is no charge to join the Rotation or to register your room.

What Does It Cost?

Registration is free forever. Placement options are shared after you register, shaped around your room and your nights — never as a bare price list up front.

Where Does The Talent Come From?

From the Rotation — verified nightlife workers across the country, starting with Florida’s I-4 Corridor. Every member is checked before they are ever shown to a venue.

How Fast Can I Get Staff?

Post your shift and review verified profiles right away. The fastest rescues are the ones posted before the weekend — Friday night at 8:40 PM is already late.

How Is Talent Verified?

Every registration is checked for complete and consistent info, the right role and the right age for the job, and no spam or fake flags. Credential proof is the venue’s requirement for its own placements — nobody is marketed or placed as licensed security without a verified Class D.

How Do I Join As Talent?

Register through the talent form and get verified to join the Rotation. Your first year is free after confirmed registration. The early ones set the standard for the whole house.

How Do Recruiters Get Paid?

Recruiters earn when the venues and talent they bring get moving — including $25 when a venue they refer becomes a paying partner. Bring the room, get paid when the room pays.

How Is Nocturnal Different From Other Hiring Sites?

Not Indeed. Not ZipRecruiter. Not Monster. Not Snagajob. We’re the crew you didn’t know you needed until Friday at 8:40 PM — nightlife only. No general job board, no endless applications from people who have never worked a bar. Just verified people who know the night.

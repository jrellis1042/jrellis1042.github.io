How A Batch Works

Verified. Batched. Exclusive.

Every batch is built the same way — from the same store, to the same standard. The difference from Data is packaging, exclusivity, and freshness.

1

Verified, One By One

Every record passes all five checks: licence active and trading, contact published or filed, email double-verified, decision-maker named where findable, human sign-off. Re-verified at least every 90 days.

2

Batched By Metro

A batch is one metro — 50 or more Verified Owner records, fresh, with the decision-maker, the fit notes, and the best channel for each venue. Pre-opening batches (licence filed, venue not yet open) are a separate, higher tier.

3

Exclusive For 30 Days

One buyer per metro. Nobody else gets that batch in that metro for the window. After the window, the records roll into the Data store. A shared lead is a footrace — this is the opposite.

What Is In A Batch

The Outreach-Ready Pack

Not a CSV dump. Every venue ships with what you need to work it today.

Verified Contact

Business email, double-verified. A catch-all result is never counted as verified.

Decision-Maker

Owner or GM name and role, wherever one exists — from a filing, the venue's own site, or a public profile.

Licence Proof

Licence type, number, and status from the public record, with the date checked. Proves the venue is legally trading.

Fit Notes

Why this venue, what it needs, what to say first — written by a human who checked the row.

Best Channel

Email, DM, or phone — the channel most likely to reach the decision-maker, per venue.

Freshness Date

Every record is stamped with its last verification date. Nothing ships stale.

What A Batch Is Not

A batch is verified access to real venues, first or exclusively. It is not meetings, replies, or sales. We never promise those — and no bought or scraped row is ever resold as-is. Every record is original research, checked by a human, and cites its source. No talent names, emails, or phone numbers are ever in a batch — venue and business data only.

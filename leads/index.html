The Nocturnal Verified Standard

Five Checks. Every Record. Every Time.

No database is verified without a second pass. Every record on the list carries all five checks, with the date and the source noted on the row — and every record is re-verified at least every 90 days.

1

It Exists And It Trades

Licence active in the public record — or a live, current public presence with a matching address. Closed venues are removed, not sold.

2

The Contact Is Published Or Filed

The email comes from the venue's own website or social pages, or the contact is named in a public filing. Never a guessed pattern presented as verified.

3

The Email Passes Two Independent Checks

Two separate verifications on every email. A catch-all result is never counted as verified.

4

The Decision-Maker Is Named Where Findable

Owner or GM name and role, from a filing, the venue's own site, or a public profile. Where none can be found, the record is tiered down honestly.

5

A Human Signed Off

A person checked the row, dated it, and noted the sources. No record ships on machine output alone.

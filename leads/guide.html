⚠ “Verified” On A Sales Page Is Not Verified In Your Inbox

Category 3 • Staffing marketing / CRM systems

Skip All Four For Lead-Gen

These sell candidate attraction and client management — none provides venue contact data. Your bottleneck is finding venue decision-makers, which none of them solve.

Bullhorn — Starter and Core tiers

ATS + recruitment CRM. The sales CRM is Pro-only (quote-only). Fits alone on Starter, but it's an ATS to manage leads you already have — only worth it later as the operating system.

Haley Marketing — Quote only

Staffing websites, SEO/PPC, career portal, marketing automation. Lead-gen is inbound — candidate applications + client enquiries, not venue clients. No monthly rate published anywhere found.

Staffing Future — Quote only

SEO career sites / job boards synced to Bullhorn + 20 ATSs; “Future Loop” reactivates your own database. FAQ states pricing depends on site, team size, and features — “you'll get a clear quote on your call.” Project + hosting model, not a small-budget tool.

Sense — Enterprise-priced

Candidate engagement / talent CRM: SMS/WhatsApp journeys, AI chatbot + Voice AI screening, database reactivation. Candidate-side only, no venue prospecting. No free plan or trial. Built for enterprise teams, not a small nightlife business.

If a CRM is needed later, Bullhorn Starter or a free HubSpot CRM are the only small-budget options — that's an ops decision, not lead-gen.

Open Questions — Flagged Plainly

  • Lusha credits conflict: 5 credits/phone + 40 free/mo (most sources) vs. 10 credits/phone + 70 free/mo (one 2026 source). Re-check lusha.com/pricing.

  • Haley & Staffing Future: no monthly rate published anywhere found.
  • Google Places licence: whether building an outreach list breaches Google Maps Platform terms was flagged in secondary sources; the terms themselves were not read in full. Read them before bulk-building a list.
  • Small-venue match rates are unknown: no source confirms coverage for small independent Florida bars/clubs in Apollo/Lusha/UpLead. The trial-allowance test against ~50 known venues is the only way to know.
  • Still unspecified: how many venues/month you want to contact, and whether “verified” means SMTP-verified, human-confirmed, or website-published — that decides which tier is enough.

How We Ran The Test

  • 1. Build the venue list: we pulled Winter Haven / Orlando / Tampa bars & clubs from the Google Places trial allowance; cross-checked each against Florida DBPR — kept only active licences (4COP for full-liquor).
  • 2. Enrich ~50 venues: we ran the same 50 through the Apollo, Hunter, and Snov trial allowances (75 / 50 / 50 credits). Compared who finds an owner/GM email.
  • 3. Verify before any send: Hunter Verifier (0.5 credit) on every address. Count bounces/unknowns per tool — that's your real match + accuracy rate.
  • 4. Decide on evidence: if Apollo's match rate holds, Apollo Basic is the first paid tool. If you need volume of generic venue inboxes instead, Scrap.io Basic is first. Don't buy both until the test says so.

Note: Steps 1–4 are how the research test was run, restated — no platform account was created during research.

Juiced Up Promotions · Services

What we do for your night. Nothing more, nothing less.

Four services, one job: put your night in front of the crowd that should be in the room. Every service is built for clubs, venues, and events — and every piece of creative is full color, in the Juiced Up look.

The Juiced Up astronaut — planting the flag for your night.

The Service Menu

Four ways to juice a night.

Mix them or take one. The offer — what each service includes and how it is charged — is still being decided, so this page describes the work, not a package or a rate.

Service 1

Event Promotion

Your night, pushed where the crowd already is — in the days before the doors open, while the week is still deciding where to go.

  • Promotion built around your night, your room, your crowd
  • Messaging and timing planned around the date, not a template
  • For club nights, one-off events, and special nights at an existing venue

Not included: any promise about how many people show up. Promotion puts the night in front of people; the night itself has to deliver.

Service 2

Social Campaigns

Short, loud, full-color campaigns across the channels the nightlife crowd actually watches.

  • Creative and copy made for the feed, not the boardroom
  • Built around the night — the room, the music, the energy
  • Every post carries the Juiced Up mark

Not included: any promise about reach, views, likes, or ticket sales. Campaigns are built to be seen; what the crowd does next is theirs.

Service 3

Flyer & Poster Creative

The art for the night — flyers and posters in the Juiced Up look, made to be spotted across a room and across a feed.

  • Flyer and poster design for print and for the feed
  • Full color, bold type, the Juiced Up characters and cup
  • Built from your night’s details — date, room, lineup, theme

Not included: printing or distribution. Creative is the design; where it goes is agreed with you.

Service 4

Night-Of Promotion

Promotion doesn’t stop at the door. The room itself is the loudest ad for the next night.

  • On-the-night energy that turns a good room into a returning crowd
  • Content captured in the room, in full color
  • The night feeding the next night’s campaign

Not included: staffing, security, door, or bar work — that is the agency’s job, not promotion’s. Juiced Up promotes the night; Nocturnal Staffing Agency staffs it.

How To Read This Page

What’s decided, what’s not.

Decided: the four services above, the Juiced Up look (full color, bold, unmistakable), and the rule that promotion never promises attendance, sales, or a packed room.

Not decided yet: how Juiced Up earns — whether from event promotion fees, door splits, venue partnerships, or another arrangement — and what each service includes as a package. Those decisions are owed, and this page will say so plainly until they are made. There are no package names here that imply a guaranteed outcome, and no rates — rates are agreed in conversation, never posted.

How A Campaign Works

Four steps. One loud night.

1
Tell us the night

The venue, the date, the room, the crowd you want in it. That’s the brief.

2
We build the push

Creative, copy, and the channels — made for your night, in the Juiced Up look.

3
It goes out

The campaign runs where the crowd is, in the days and hours before the doors open.

4
The room does the rest

Night-of promotion keeps the energy up and gives the next night something to come back for.

What a campaign never promises: attendance, ticket sales, or a packed room. Promotion puts the night in front of people; the night itself has to deliver. No guarantees, ever.

How Verification Works

Verified before, during, and after every placement

Reliability isn't a promise here — it's a process. Both sides confirm the same facts at every stage, so a placement only counts when everyone agrees it happened the way it was supposed to.

Before the night
  • Both sides confirm the same facts up front: role, date, time, place, dress code, and agreed pay
  • Credentials and eligibility are verified before anyone is matched
  • The worker confirms they have transport — and a backup is named
During the night
  • The worker checks in on arrival; the venue confirms the arrival
  • Either side can flag an issue with one tap, the moment it happens
  • If a no-show happens, the named backup gets the offer first
After the night
  • Both sides confirm hours worked and completion
  • Both sides rate the experience
  • The placement counts only when both sides sign off satisfied

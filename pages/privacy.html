Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Nocturnal Staffing Agency ("Nocturnal," "we," "us") runs a nightlife staffing service at nocturnalstaffing.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By registering with us or submitting one of our forms — including a Facebook or Instagram lead form — you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Registration information: your name, email address, phone number, city and state, the role you work or the type of venue you operate, your availability, and any experience or certifications you tell us about.
  • Lead form information: when you submit a Facebook or Instagram lead form, we receive the details you enter there — typically your name, email address, phone number, venue name, and city.
  • Communications: emails, messages, and calls between you and us, including replies to our outreach.
  • Basic technical information: standard web logs from our hosting providers (such as pages visited and browser type). We do not run third-party advertising trackers on our registration forms.

How We Use It

  • To process and verify registrations for talent and venues.
  • To contact you about your registration, verification, shift postings, and staffing requests.
  • To match verified talent with venues that are hiring, as part of the staffing service you registered for.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our service, and to keep records of our outreach and placements.

What We Never Do

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • We never post your name, email, or phone number publicly as part of our marketing.

When We Share It

We share information only as needed to run the service: with the venues or talent involved in a staffing match you are part of, and with the service providers that operate our website, email, and customer records (such as our hosting, email, and CRM providers). We may also disclose information if required by law.

How Long We Keep It

We keep registration and communication records while your registration is active and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see Your Choices).

Your Choices

  • Opt out: reply "stop" or ask us at any time and we will stop outreach to you.
  • Access or deletion: email us and we will provide or delete your personal information, subject to any legal record-keeping duties.
  • Corrections: if anything we hold is wrong, tell us and we will fix it.

Security

We use reputable providers and limit access to registration records to the people running the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care with what you share with us.

Children

Our service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we do not place anyone under 18 into alcohol service.

Changes To This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

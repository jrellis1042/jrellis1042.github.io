Recruiter Portal

Recruit nightlife workers, refer them to register free, review their credentials, and endorse the ones you stand behind. A recruiter endorsement is not verification — verification still goes through Nocturnal's verifier, and no candidate is verified until that step is complete.

Registration is free for talent and venues at nocturnalstaffing.com. No prices are listed on this page.

2. Report a referral

Use this after you have sent a candidate to register. This form does not register them — they must still register themselves. It only attributes the referral to you.

3. Request a change to this portal

Recruiters may request edits to this portal any way they wish. Requests are logged for review and applied through the normal process. Requests that would publish pricing, name the owner in public copy, verify talent without the verifier, create app accounts, move deals, or promise placements or pay are flagged for the owner first and are not applied automatically.

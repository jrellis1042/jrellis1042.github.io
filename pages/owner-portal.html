What We Will Sell

Unit 1 — Venue lead lists (by city / region / venue type): business name, city, state, venue type, verified business email, fit score, last-verified date, source. No phone numbers in v1. No suppressed contacts, ever. Sold as a dated snapshot.

Unit 2 — Aggregated demand reports: no per-venue rows. Short-staffed cities, hard-to-fill roles, territory coverage, trend lines. This is the first unit that can ship — it contains no contact data at all.

Unit 3 (later) — Enrichment / verification: a buyer brings their own venue list; Nocturnal verifies contacts and scores against its coverage.

Backfill addresses/websites from public sources only. 8. Record provenance per record.

Proposed Launch Gates (Proposals — Not Decided)

Hard Boundary — Talent Data Is Never For Sale

Talent PII is never for sale. No talent names, emails, phones, or dates of birth in any data product, ever, without explicit opt-in consent (none exists today). Only venue and business data is in scope. Any step that would touch talent records is skipped.
